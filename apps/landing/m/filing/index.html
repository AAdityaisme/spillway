Part II / The Instruments

Four instruments of governance.

§1 · Budgets that block

The limit is the law.

Limits cascade from organization to team to key and are enforced at the gateway. An over-budget request is refused before the spend exists¹ — the 402 arrives instead of the invoice.

Part IV / Notes to the Statement

Notes and basis of preparation.

¹ Budget checks evaluate at org → team → key before the upstream call. A blocked request receives HTTP 402 and never reaches the provider; the amount stated in red is the spend that would otherwise have been incurred.
² Approval chains support quorums (all / any / N), time-boxed delegation, and expiry. Decisions are recorded with approver identity and cryptographic reference in the audit log.
³ Anomaly baselines are computed per scope from weekday-matched history; variances are scored and, where enabled, the offending key is suspended automatically pending review.
⁴ Routing selects the least-cost model meeting the configured quality threshold; savings are stated against the frontier-model price for the same request.
⁵ Tenant isolation is enforced with row-level security in the database. Provider keys are sealed with AES-256-GCM; virtual keys are hashed and shown once.
⁶ Request bodies are retained only with explicit opt-in, subject to per-organization retention windows. Chargeback and audit exports are available on the Governance tier. SOC 2 examination in progress.
