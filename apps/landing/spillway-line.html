Objections, answered

The questions every proxy buyer asks.

What happens if Spillway goes down?

Requests fail open: if the gateway is ever unreachable, your client can fall back to the provider directly and your product keeps working. We sit in the path to govern spend, not to hold your traffic hostage.

Do you store my prompts and responses?

Not by default. Request and response bodies are dropped after the decision; metadata (tokens, cost, route, verdict) is what we keep. Body retention is explicit opt-in, per org, with a retention window you control.

Does streaming and tool-calling still work?

Yes. SSE streams, function calling, JSON mode, and vision payloads pass through untouched. The policy check happens before the request leaves the gateway; after that, bytes flow straight through.

Which providers are behind the endpoint?

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Azure OpenAI, Bedrock, Mistral, and Cohere, plus anything OpenAI-compatible: vLLM, Ollama, TGI, or your own gateway. One key, one bill, one policy layer.

Can we run it in our region, or on-prem?

The Enterprise tier ships in-region and on-prem deployments with SSO/SAML, SCIM, and an SLA. The policy engine is the same one the cloud runs.

Why not just use each provider's spend limits?

Per-provider caps are monthly, coarse, and post-hoc; they cannot cascade org → team → key, cannot require an approval, and cannot see your other providers. A control plane enforces one policy across all of them, before the spend.
